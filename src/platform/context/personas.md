title: Endless Prototyping Playground audiences
description: Endless Prototyping Playground audiences guidance for the Endless team.
---

These are working audience profiles approved for this studio, not validated research.

## Endless designers

Designers direct agents to explore flows with Endless Studios' real components, named states, documents and canvases. They need reliable previews, clear simulated data and links they can share for review. Success is a reviewable flow whose states and assumptions are visible without understanding platform internals.

## Endless Studios team

Engineers and teammates review prototypes for behavior, feasibility and intent. They need recognizable production components, preserved copied-source provenance and explicit gaps between a sample and production behavior. Success is understanding the proposed experience and what still needs a production decision.

## Agents

Agents implement the designer's intent and verify results. They need discoverable system context, scoped file operations, deterministic commands and actionable failures. Success is preserved work, passing relevant checks and a result the person can inspect.

Use the person's actual goals when available. Product audience guidance belongs to the assigned system; these profiles describe people working with this studio.
